CHAPTER 3: BRIMLEY FORGE AND ATLAS
- Gavin introduces himself: Gavin Holloway, lives in Brimley Forge, New Mexico
- Town history lesson during truck ride:
  * Originally called Gristle Creek - dusty supply post
  * 1829: Elias Crump discovered silver - town boomed (silver rush)
  * 1840s: Not much silver, but LOTS of iron ore
  * Jonathan Brimley built biggest steel factory - town renamed after him
  * Brimley's Forge: Where "metal is born" - melting iron, making steel
  * Trip hammers: Giant mechanical hammers that forged the steel (WHAM WHAM WHAM)
  * Made: steel rails, tools, bridge beams, horseshoes, weapon parts (barrel blanks, cannon wheel rings)
  * Manufacturing philosophy: Each factory makes specific parts really well
- Town geography: Split between north (new, expensive) and south (old, cheaper, has "characters")
- Gavin's family moved to south side because it's cheaper
- Brimley's Forge building stopped production in late 1960s
- Building sat empty for decades
- ~15 years ago: Marvin Marindale (richest person in town, now deceased) converted it into a library
- The Atlas Library: 5 stories tall, brick building, iron-framed windows
- Parking lot is nearly empty - not many people know about/use this treasure
- Gavin feels content for first time in months - happy to be where he is
